What is Cricket?

Cricket is a popular team sport played with a bat and a ball. Two teams play against each other, and each team usually has 11 players. Cricket is widely played and followed in many countries around the world.

🏏 Formats of Cricket

Test Cricket: The traditional format of cricket, which can be played for up to five days.

One Day International (ODI): Each team normally gets 50 overs to bat.

T20 Cricket: Each team gets 20 overs, making it a shorter and faster format of the game.

⭐ Important Roles in Cricket

Cricket has different player roles such as batsman, bowler, wicketkeeper and all-rounder.

Each player contributes to the team's performance in a different way.

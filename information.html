📋 Basic Rules of Cricket

  • A cricket team usually consists of 11 players.
  • A cricket match is played between two teams.
  • The batsman tries to score as many runs as possible.
  • The bowler tries to dismiss the batsman and restrict the number of runs scored.
  • The fielding team tries to stop the batsmen from scoring runs and takes wickets.

🏃 Scoring in Cricket

A batsman scores runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets.

If the batsman hits the ball over the boundary without the ball touching the ground, it is called a six and six runs are awarded.

If the ball reaches the boundary after touching the ground, it is called a four and four runs are awarded.

🎯 Main Roles in Cricket

Batsman: A player who specializes in scoring runs for the team.

Bowler: A player who delivers the ball and tries to dismiss the batsman.

Wicketkeeper: A player who stands behind the wickets and catches or stops the ball when it passes the batsman.

All-rounder: A player who contributes to the team with both batting and bowling.
